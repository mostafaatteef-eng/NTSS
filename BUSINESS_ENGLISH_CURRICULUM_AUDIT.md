2. **Year 1 source-year mismatch is intentional:** Term 1 is the uploaded 2025 first-term book while Term 2 is the uploaded 2024 second-term book. Do not represent these files as one matched annual edition.
3. **Do not invent Term 1 / Term 2 labels for Years 2 and 3.** Unit numbers alone do not prove a term; the supplied source covers do not explicitly identify a term.

5. **Keep Lessons 6–8 separate from the five taught lessons:** Revision, Evaluation of Unit’s Project, Exam.
